Shop-Based

Diesel Technician

Diagnose, maintain, and repair medium- and heavy-duty diesel trucks and trailers in our Atlanta-area shop.

Responsibilities
  • Diagnose and repair engine, transmission, electrical, and brake systems
  • Perform DOT inspections and preventive maintenance service
  • Document diagnostics and repairs accurately
  • Keep your work area clean, safe, and organized
Qualifications
  • Experience with medium- or heavy-duty diesel trucks
  • Own basic hand tools; shop provides diagnostic equipment
  • Valid driver's license
  • ASE or manufacturer certifications a plus, not required
On The Road

Mobile Service Technician

Respond to roadside breakdowns and on-site service calls across the Atlanta area in a fully equipped service vehicle.

Responsibilities
  • Travel to customer locations and roadside breakdowns to diagnose and repair
  • Perform on-site repairs including brakes, electrical, and tires
  • Communicate ETA and repair status with dispatch and customers
  • Maintain and stock the service vehicle
Qualifications
  • Experience with diesel truck/trailer repair; roadside experience a plus
  • Valid driver's license with a clean driving record
  • Comfortable working independently, including some nights/weekends
  • Own basic hand tools and strong customer communication skills
